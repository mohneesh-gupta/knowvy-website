
# Azure Tech Group Bhopal – Comprehensive Code of Conduct

## 1. Purpose & Core Philosophy

The core mission of **Azure Tech Group Bhopal** is to foster a vibrant, welcoming, and collaborative community dedicated to exploring the Microsoft Data Platform, cloud technologies, and data engineering. We believe that a diverse community brings diverse perspectives, which ultimately drives innovation and learning.

Accordingly, we are dedicated to providing a harassment-free, safe, and inclusive experience for everyone, regardless of:

* Gender, gender identity, or gender expression
* Sexual orientation
* Disability or neurodiversity
* Physical appearance, body size, or race
* Age, religion, or nationality
* Professional background or technology choices (e.g., beginner vs. expert, open-source vs. proprietary tools)

We do not tolerate harassment, discrimination, or exclusionary behavior toward community members, speakers, volunteers, or organizers in any form.

---

## 2. Scope & Applicability

This Code of Conduct applies to all participants in our community and governs all interactions across our official spaces. This includes:

* **In-Person Events:** Local meetups, workshops, hackathons, and social gatherings.
* **Virtual & Hybrid Events:** Webinars, live streams, online presentations, and virtual Q&A sessions.
* **Digital Platforms:** Discussion forums, WhatsApp/Telegram groups, LinkedIn posts, GitHub repositories, and any online channels associated with **Knowvy Technologies** or the user group.
* **Direct Communication:** One-on-one professional messaging, emails, or conversations between community members that stem from group activities.

---

## 3. Expected Professional Behavior

To ensure a positive environment, all leaders, speakers, partners, and attendees are expected to uphold the following standards:

* **Exercise Consideration and Respect:** Treat everyone with kindness and dignity. Respect others' time, boundaries, and varying perspectives.
* **Foster Inclusivity:** Actively welcome newcomers, students, and those transitioning into tech. Help create an environment where individuals feel safe asking basic or "simple" questions.
* **Communicate Collaboratively:** Focus on constructive criticism and data-driven discussions rather than personal attacks or ideological arguments.
* **Promote Professional Growth:** Share knowledge generously. When offering feedback on presentations or code snippets, do so with the intent to help the other person improve.
* **Be Mindful of Your Surroundings:** If you notice a dangerous situation, someone in distress, or a clear violation of these guidelines, please alert the leadership team immediately.

---

## 4. Unacceptable Behavior & Harassment

Harassment includes, but is not limited to, offensive verbal or written comments, deliberate intimidation, or actions that make others feel unsafe or unwelcome. Specific examples of prohibited behavior include:

* **Discriminatory Language:** Offensive remarks related to race, gender, religion, age, or disability.
* **Inappropriate Presentations:** The use of sexualized images, language, nudity, or crude jokes in presentation slides, chat windows, or promotional materials.
* **Disruptive Conduct:** Sustained or intentional disruption of talks, workshops, or online chats (such as "trolling" or spamming).
* **Unwelcome Contact:** Unsolicited sexual attention, inappropriate physical contact, or stalking/following participants at physical venues.
* **Doxxing & Privacy Violations:** Sharing someone else’s private data (such as home addresses, personal phone numbers, or private emails) without their explicit, prior consent.
* **Aggressive Advocacy:** Berating or insulting individuals based on their choice of technology, operating system, or past technical background.

---

## 5. Reporting Procedures & Incident Management

If you experience harassment, notice that someone else is facing unacceptable behavior, or have any other safety concerns, please reach out to our leadership team as soon as possible.

All reports will be handled with the utmost confidentiality, sensitivity, and respect for the reporter’s privacy.

* **Primary Contact:** Mohneesh Gupta (User Group Primary Leader)
* **Direct Email Address:** work.mohneesh@gmail.com
* **How to Report:** You can contact us via email, send a direct message on LinkedIn, or approach an organizer privately during an in-person event. Please include as many details as possible (e.g., date, time, location, platform, screenshots, or witnesses) to help us review the matter effectively.

---

## 6. Enforcement & Consequences

Upon receiving a report, the leadership team will review the details fairly and swiftly. Anyone asked by an organizer to stop unacceptable behavior is expected to comply immediately.

If a participant engages in behavior that violates this Code of Conduct, the **Azure Tech Group Bhopal** leadership reserves the right to take any action deemed necessary to preserve the safety of the community. Sanctions may include:

1. A formal verbal or written warning.
2. Immediate removal from a specific presentation, meeting, or virtual call.
3. A permanent ban from all future events, online forums, and digital channels.
4. Reporting egregious violations or unlawful behavior to local law enforcement or to the primary [Microsoft Azure Data Community](https://aka.ms/AzureDataCommunity/CodeOfConduct) compliance team.

---

## 7. Attribution & Alignment

 document is structured to strictly align with the global values of the [Microsoft Azure Data Community Code of Conduct](https://aka.ms/AzureDataCommunity/CodeOfConduct) and derives principles from the widely adopted [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/). It serves as our foundational framework for maintaining a healthy technical community.
